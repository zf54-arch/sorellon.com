What we do

A supply plan built around your specification

We match the requirement to an evidenced product route, then set out the assumptions that affect price, lead time and delivery.

01 · Source

Specification-led sourcing

We identify suitable UK and international suppliers against each buyer's specification and delivery requirements.

02 · Check

Evidence and compliance

CE/UKCA evidence, technical files, safety data sheets and traceability are requested where the specification requires them.

03 · Plan

Supply planning

Lead times, call-off schedules and multi-site delivery are coordinated with appointed suppliers and carriers.

04 · Support

One UK contact

A named contact provides clear updates from the initial enquiry through delivery and document handover.

How it works

Four checkpoints from specification to delivery

We make the next action and any dependency visible at each stage.

1

Review the specification

Confirm the product, quantities, acceptance criteria, delivery points and schedule.

2

Check suppliers and evidence

Obtain product and supplier documentation; coordinate quotations and samples as required.

3

Approve and plan

We confirm the schedule, paperwork and any agreed assumptions.

4

Deliver and support

UK delivery to one or many sites with status updates and records.

Supply categories

A broad range, assessed requirement by requirement

Examples include footwear, consumables, components, machinery, trailers and equipment. We assess suitability, availability and evidence before confirming supply. Sorellon does not manufacture products or hold every product in stock.

Consumables

Disposable wipes, gloves, disinfectants and PPE where evidence is available.

Footwear and workwear

Materials, sizing, performance and sample requirements matched to the specification.

Components and tools

Compatibility, dimensions and technical evidence reviewed for the intended application.

Machinery, trailers and equipment

Capacity, safety documentation and delivery needs assessed; installation or servicing separately scoped.

Contract requirements

Eligibility and supporting evidence

We review the buyer's procurement conditions, evidence requirements and delivery obligations for each opportunity. Any gaps or dependencies are raised before commitments are made. No framework appointment is implied.
