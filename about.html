What Sorellon does

Sorellon Limited supplies goods to UK public-sector organisations. We focus on cleaning, hygiene, healthcare consumables and maintenance essentials.

We quote against the buyer's specification through a vetted global supplier network. Each proposal sets out the product, pack format, lead time, delivery plan and available supporting evidence.

We are B2B only. We do not claim a standard, certificate or framework appointment unless it can be evidenced.
