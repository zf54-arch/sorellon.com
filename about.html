About Sorellon Limited

A UK company providing specification-led sourcing and general goods supply.

What Sorellon does

Sorellon Limited is a general goods procurement and supply business for contracting authorities and commercial organisations. Requirements may range from footwear and consumables to components, machinery, trailers and equipment.

We quote against the buyer's specification through suitable UK and international suppliers. Each proposal sets out the product, pack format, lead time, delivery plan and available supporting evidence.

We supply to organisations and source against each contract rather than manufacturing products or holding a universal stock range. Standards, certification and framework appointments are stated only where supported by evidence.
