Equality and Diversity Policy

Policy owner: Director. Reviewed annually.

Our position

Sorellon Limited is committed to fair treatment and equal opportunity. We do not accept unlawful discrimination, harassment, bullying or victimisation.

Scope

This policy covers directors, workers, applicants, suppliers, subcontractors, customers and other people we deal with.

How we apply it

  • Make work and supplier decisions on relevant, objective criteria.
  • Do not discriminate because of age, disability, gender reassignment, marriage or civil partnership, pregnancy or maternity, race, religion or belief, sex or sexual orientation.
  • Make reasonable adjustments for disabled people where required.
  • Use clear reporting routes and deal with concerns promptly and fairly.
  • Ask suppliers and subcontractors to meet applicable equality law.

Reporting a concern

Concerns can be sent in confidence to sales@sorellon.com. Retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith is not accepted.

Responsibility and review

The Director is responsible for implementation. The policy is reviewed annually and when relevant law or business arrangements change.
