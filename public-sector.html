Where we fit

Sorellon Limited sources and supplies general goods against the specifications of contracting authorities and commercial organisations.

Requirements are assessed individually, including the procurement conditions, required product evidence, quantities and delivery schedule. Sorellon does not claim appointment to a purchasing framework without supporting evidence.

We work from the buyer's specification with suitable UK and international suppliers. Product declarations, test reports, SDS and traceability are supplied where they apply and are available.

Examples of requirements assessed

  • Footwear and workwear
  • Consumables and general goods
  • Components and tools
  • Machinery and equipment
  • Trailers and site equipment
  • Multi-site and repeat delivery
