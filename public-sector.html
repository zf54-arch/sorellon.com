Where we fit

Sorellon supplies cleaning, hygiene, healthcare and maintenance goods to public bodies and framework contractors.

We are preparing to compete through the Government Commercial Agency, NHS Supply Chain, ESPO, YPO, local authority frameworks and open dynamic markets. This page does not claim that Sorellon is appointed to any of them.

We work from the buyer's specification through a vetted global supplier network. Product declarations, test reports, SDS and traceability are supplied where they apply and are available.

Typical requirements

  • Wipes and hand-hygiene products
  • Janitorial and cleaning products
  • PPE and healthcare consumables
  • Paper, signage and facilities essentials
  • Painting and maintenance kits
  • Multi-site and repeat delivery
