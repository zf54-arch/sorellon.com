Sorellon Limited — Capability Statement

Company No. 16575318 · England & Wales · General Goods Supply

Company overview

Sorellon Limited is a UK general goods procurement and supply business, incorporated in England and Wales on 10 July 2025. Company number: 16575318.

We review buyer specifications, identify suitable UK and international suppliers, check relevant documentation and coordinate quotations, samples, logistics and delivery. A named UK contact maintains communication with the contracting authority or commercial buyer.

Requirements may include footwear, consumables, components, machinery, trailers, equipment and other general goods. This breadth is supported by a consistent sourcing process: suitability and supply arrangements are assessed for each contract.

Sorellon is a supplier, not a manufacturer. Products are sourced against specific requirements; availability and lead times are confirmed with suppliers rather than assumed to be held in stock.

Specification-led sourcing

  1. Review the requirement. Confirm specification, quantities, applicable standards, acceptance criteria and delivery schedule.
  2. Identify suppliers. Assess UK and international options against product suitability, available evidence, commercial terms and lead time.
  3. Check documentation. Obtain relevant product and supplier records, identify gaps and coordinate samples or additional checks where required and available.
  4. Confirm the proposal. Set out the proposed product, price, assumptions, delivery arrangements and any unresolved dependencies.
  5. Coordinate delivery. Maintain buyer communication, track agreed milestones and provide the required delivery records.

Product evidence and limitations

  • Technical data, applicable CE/UKCA evidence, safety data sheets, test reports and traceability are checked where relevant to the product and contract.
  • Supplier claims are not treated as independent certification or inspection evidence.
  • Samples, independent testing and inspection are subject to availability, agreed scope and cost.
  • Alternatives are identified clearly and require buyer agreement before substitution.
  • Independent ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certification is not currently held. Cyber Essentials certification is not currently held.
  • Insurance certificates are not currently available. Contract-specific eligibility and evidence requirements must be resolved before commitments are made.

Logistics and contract delivery

Delivery plans are agreed against the contract's locations, quantities and schedule. Freight, customs arrangements and UK delivery can be coordinated with appropriate logistics providers. Consolidation, warehousing, scheduled call-offs and stockholding depend on the contract, supplier availability and agreed finance.

Installation, commissioning, servicing or specialist approvals are assessed separately where a requirement includes them. They are not assumed to form part of a goods-only supply.

Supplier due diligence

Sorellon does not claim framework appointments or public-sector contract history without supporting evidence. Current supplier facts and limitations are set out on our public-sector information page.

Our published environmental, equality and diversity, modern slavery, health and safety, privacy and carbon reduction policies support buyer review. The first evidence-backed emissions baseline is not yet complete; no verified emissions reduction is claimed.
