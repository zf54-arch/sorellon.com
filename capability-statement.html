Sorellon Limited — Capability Statement

UK public-sector supply · Sourcing, documentation and delivery

About Us

Sorellon Limited is a UK-based goods supplier for government, healthcare, education and other public-sector buyers. We work to clear specifications, disclose dependencies and provide the product evidence available for each requirement.

Our approach is simple: straightforward products, serious delivery. Every order is managed by a named UK contact with full accountability from enquiry to delivery, supported by a responsive global supply chain and a strict focus on documentation accuracy and performance reporting.

Sorellon is an early-stage supplier. We do not claim public-sector contract history that we cannot evidence. Our current focus is building a documented, bid-ready supply chain and competing first for proportionate, product-led opportunities.

Mission & Values

  • Integrity: Honest pricing, traceable supply and open communication.
  • Responsiveness: Quotes within 24 hours, status updates throughout fulfilment.
  • Reliability: Document-controlled quotes, orders, delivery records, and specification-matched QA evidence.
  • Accountability: A named UK contact — no call centres, no delays.

Core Capabilities

1) Product Supply and Procurement Fit

  • A vetted global supplier network, assessed against each requirement.
  • Active monitoring of Find a Tender, Contracts Finder, devolved portals, and local-authority opportunities.
  • Product research and technical data collation for tender compliance.
  • Supplier due diligence, ethical checks and sustainability evidence where required.

2) Compliance & Quality Assurance

  • Independent ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certification is not currently held; certification status is disclosed accurately in every bid.
  • CE/UKCA evidence, safety data sheets, and certificates of conformity are obtained where the product and specification require them.
  • Supplier, batch, and delivery traceability requirements are agreed before quotation or award.
  • Samples, third-party inspection, and test reports can be arranged where available and proportionate.

3) Logistics & Fulfilment

  • Third-party UK warehousing and consolidation can be arranged for multi-line orders.
  • Air and sea freight, customs clearance, and duties can be coordinated through appointed logistics partners.
  • Stockholding and scheduled call-off options are subject to contract, forecast, and agreed finance.
  • Tracked delivery and proof of delivery are specified with the selected carrier.

4) Contract Readiness & Procurement Experience

  • Registered on core UK procurement portals and building evidence through live market engagement and proportionate RFQs.
  • Flexible on terms: standard 30-day payment, longer for framework authorities.
  • An information-security policy is maintained; Cyber Essentials certification is not currently held.
  • Portal, policy, and product-documentation gaps are disclosed rather than represented as completed.

5) Product Categories

  • Medical & Hygiene: Disposable wipes, syringes, catheters, gloves, disinfectants, PPE.
  • Facilities & Janitorial: Cleaning kits, bin liners, mops, buckets, floor consumables.
  • Education & Office: Stationery, teaching aids, storage solutions, safety products.
  • Infrastructure & Trade: Paint kits, tools, tapes, protective coatings, light consumables.
  • Sustainability Line: Eco-friendly wipes, biodegradable packaging, and low-carbon shipping options.

Added Value Services

  • Custom packaging and private labelling for framework or brand compliance.
  • Consolidated invoicing for simplified procurement processing.
  • On-demand technical datasheets and sample submission for evaluation.
  • Product substitution and equivalent matching to meet specification or budget targets.

Key Differentiators

  • Public Sector Focus: Built exclusively for institutional buyers.
  • Fast Triage: Initial RFQ response within one business day; delivery dates are confirmed only after supplier and freight checks.
  • Evidence First: Product claims are supported by the documents required for the specific contract.
  • Low Overheads: A lean structure supports competitive, transparent pricing.
  • Flexible Supply: Options from a vetted global supplier network are evaluated for quality, evidence, landed cost and lead time.

Sustainability & Social Value

  • Supporting the UK government's net zero and local growth agenda.
  • Requesting consolidated and lower-emission freight options where practical; no offset claim is made without evidence.
  • Prioritising recyclable and biodegradable materials where viable.
  • Promoting ethical labour and fair-trade practices in supplier selection.
  • Supporting small British manufacturers through blended supply models.

Our Promise

  • Rapid Response: Quotes within 24 hours.
  • Transparency: Clear, fixed pricing and open communication.
  • Reliability: Clear responsibility from quotation through delivery, with dependencies disclosed.
  • Compliance: Specification-relevant evidence supplied before commitments are made.
  • Accountability: Named UK contact throughout every project.
