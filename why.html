Our approach to supply

Clear requirements, proportionate evidence checks and accountable communication.

A consistent process across product categories

Sorellon sources general goods against each buyer's specification. UK and international supplier options are assessed for suitability, documentation, commercial terms and delivery requirements.

Evidence before commitments

We obtain and check relevant product and supplier documentation, identify gaps and coordinate clarification or samples where appropriate. Product claims and supply dependencies are made clear in the proposal.

Contract-specific delivery

Quantities, locations, schedules and acceptance requirements inform the delivery plan. A named UK contact coordinates supplier and logistics updates and maintains communication with the buyer.

Company information for review

Our supplier information, capability statement and published policies set out our current position, including limitations.
