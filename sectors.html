Sectors and buyer requirements

General goods sourcing for public-sector and commercial requirements, assessed against each buyer's specification.

Sectors

The sectors below illustrate the requirements we can assess. They do not imply existing clients, awarded contracts or framework appointments.

NHS & Healthcare

Consumables, equipment and general goods assessed against applicable safety, traceability and product evidence requirements.

Local Authorities

Goods, machinery, trailers and equipment for estates and services, subject to the specified operational and delivery requirements.

Education

Consumables, equipment and supplies for schools and universities, with suitability and delivery schedules confirmed.

Central Government

General goods and components assessed against departmental specifications and contract documentation requirements.

Housing & Facilities

Tools, components, consumables and equipment matched to maintenance requirements and delivery locations.

Emergency Services

Footwear, consumables and equipment subject to the buyer's performance, safety and acceptance criteria.

Defence & MoD

General goods assessed against product specifications, eligibility conditions and traceability requirements.

Transport

Components, equipment and consumables assessed for compatibility, documentation and delivery needs.

Commercial & Other

Other general goods requirements reviewed for supplier suitability, evidence, samples and achievable lead times.

A consistent process across sectors

The product category may vary; specification review, supplier checks, clear documentation and agreed delivery requirements remain central.
