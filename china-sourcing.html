The honest boundary

This sprint is desk research and commercial triage. It helps you decide which conversations deserve time and money; it is not a substitute for samples, independent inspection, laboratory testing, legal advice or a signed freight quote.

  • Supplier claims are labelled as claims until supporting evidence is checked.
  • Landed-cost figures are scenarios until the commodity code, shipment and Incoterm are confirmed.
  • Sorellon will not call a supplier “verified” based only on an online badge or listing.
  • Goods, samples, audits, inspection and freight booking are separately scoped.
